# Dovetail selection and evidence

Checked 24 October 2026. Family: research-evidence. No build in this registry has this family. Thematic covers response-theme analytics; this build covers study provenance, exact quotations, source-use review and finding-to-decision follow-through. It does not repeat tonight's Microsoft 365 governance family.

## Annual bill first

GBP 12,508.43 annualised historical spend, calculated as half of the GBP 25,016.85 excluding VAT two-year award to Dovetail Research Pty Ltd. [UK contract-details notice](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/012596-2025) covers 2 April 2025 to 1 April 2027. [Award notice](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/010980-2025/PDF) reports the same total. The primary notice was search-indexed this run; direct PDF requests returned 403. [Structured record mirror](https://d3tenders.com/contract/?ocid=ocds-h6vhtk-04efae) supplies the dates and total for cross-checking. This is not a current price, an NZ/AU invoice or evidence of a small-business contract. No currency conversion is claimed.

[Dovetail pricing](https://dovetail.com/pricing/) currently lists Free and Enterprise, with Enterprise priced privately. The landing page therefore gives no subscription amount. Do not turn the historical contract into a public tariff.

## Export finding

[Current export documentation](https://docs.dovetail.com/help/projects/download-project-data) describes readable CSV for raw data and Docs. It now describes encrypted full-project archives. Older search snippets describe JSONL; those snippets are stale and were not used for the importer. The synthetic fixture exercises header mapping and multiline text. It is not a vendor-provided sample.
